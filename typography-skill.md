# Vektor Premium Typography System

This document outlines the typography rules and constraints to maintain an editorial, cinematic, and premium feel for the Vektor public landing pages, blog, and docs.

## 1. Font Selection

- **Editorial Headings (H1, H2, H3):** _Playfair Display_ (or _PP Editorial New_ in future), a high-contrast serif with classic elegance.
- **UI & Body Copy:** _Plus Jakarta Sans_ or _Geist Sans_, geometric, sharp, modern sans-serifs with high readability.

## 2. Weight Strategy

- **Avoid Heavy Weights:** Do not use `800` (Extra Bold) or default `bold` for standard UI elements, form labels, buttons, or list numbers. They create visual clutter and look heavy/unpolished.
- **Buttons & Navigation:** Use `500` (Medium) or `600` (Semibold) for text to look crisp and precise.
- **Eyebrows & Kickers:** Use `600` (Semibold) in uppercase with letter-spacing (e.g., `0.05em` to `0.08em`).
- **Body Text:** Use `400` (Regular) or `300` (Light) to let headings command attention.
- **Headings:** Use `400` (Regular) for serif headers to maintain an authoritative, classic editorial look.

## 3. CSS Reference Variables

- `--font-sans`: `"Plus Jakarta Sans", "Geist Sans", "SF Pro", -apple-system, sans-serif`
- `--font-serif`: `"Playfair Display", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
